# Third-party material

## Fonts and template assets
Templates, their fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1, from Fontsource / Google
Fonts) and their CC0 / public-domain 3D assets are not stored in this
repository. They are kept in a private repository together with their licence
and credit files, and are used under those licences.
